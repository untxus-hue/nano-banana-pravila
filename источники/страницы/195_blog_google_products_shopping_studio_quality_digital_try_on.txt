Generate a digital version of yourself to virtually try on clothes. 

Lilian Rincon 
VP of Product, Consumer Shopping 

Dec 11, 2025 

Say goodbye to bad dressing room lighting and awkward outfit selfies. Today U.S. shoppers have a new way to use our virtual try on tool : Now if you don’t have a full body photo of yourself you can use a selfie and Nano Banana , our Gemini 2.5 Flash Image model, will generate a full body digital version of you for virtual try on. 
Simply upload a selfie at g.co/shop/tryon , select your usual clothing size and we’ll generate several studio-like images. Select one to make it your default try on photo, and start shopping. If you prefer, you still have the option to virtually try on clothes using a full body photo or a range of models with diverse body types. 
Make your image now at google.com/shopping/tryon , and start trying on clothes from billions of product listings from our Shopping Graph.
