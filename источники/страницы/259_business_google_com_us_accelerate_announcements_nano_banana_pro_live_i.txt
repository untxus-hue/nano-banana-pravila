Nano Banana Pro is Live in Google Ads!
Introducing Nano Banana Pro: Our Most Advanced Image Generation Model, Now in Google Ads.
Social Module
Share
Copy link
×
We’re thrilled to announce that Nano Banana Pro, our most advanced image generation model, is now rolling out to all active Google Ads users in Asset Studio. Nano Banana Pro brings a new era of functional, high-fidelity design directly into your campaign workflow, empowering you to create compelling, on-brand visuals that drive results.
With Nano Banana Pro, you can:
Engage in conversational editing
: Simply ask for the edits you want, and Nano Banana Pro will deliver. Create seasonal variations of your top-performing ads or test new creative hypotheses without starting from scratch.
Maintain product fidelity and achieve high realism
: Showcase your products with a new level of fine detail and photorealistic accuracy, keeping them intact from the original asset. Nano Banana Pro excels at rendering complex textures and can generate sharp, legible text on product labels and packaging.
Create compelling showcases with multiple products:
Instantly create a single, cohesive lifestyle scene or product showcase featuring up to five of your products.
Get started with Nano Banana Pro today in
Asset Studio
and see the difference for yourself!
