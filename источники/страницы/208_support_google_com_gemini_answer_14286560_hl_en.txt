Generate & edit images with Gemini Apps
You can create captivating images in seconds with Nano Banana 2. From work, play, or anything in between, Gemini Apps can help you generate and refine images to help bring your imagination to life. For paid subscribers, Nano Banana Pro is also available to regenerate images.
Important:
When you generate images with Gemini Apps, remember that you agreed to Google's
Terms of Service
, including the
Prohibited Use Policy
. Be sure not to violate others' copyright or privacy rights. Use your discretion before you rely on, publish, or use content that Gemini Apps generate. Users with a work or school account may be subject to different terms depending on the license type. Learn more about how to
use Gemini Apps with your work or school Google Account
.

Key features of Nano Banana 2
Improved world knowledge:
create diagrams and infographics with more accurate info.
Character consistency:
Maintain the look of a person or character across various generated images, including the ability to combine multiple images to blend scenes or ideas.
Local edits:
Making quick and specific changes to parts of an image.
Better text rendering:
Add text to images with more accurate spelling, for all supported languages.
Enhanced instruction following:
The model can follow more complex requests, to capture details.
High output quality:
Preview images at high resolution. Download images at at 2K resolution with a Google AI plan or at 1K without an AI plan.
Tips:
Nano Banana 2 is ideal to create personalized images such as when you want to view yourself with different hairstyles, picture yourself in different scenes or create memes.
If you’re in the US and meet other requirements, Nano Banana 2 can also use Personal Intelligence to create and edit images tailored entirely to you.

About Nano Banana models
To use a different Nano Banana model, switch your Gemini model before you create your image request.
Learn how to switch models
.
Nano Banana offers different models designed for various needs.
Nano Banana 2 Lite:
This option is used when the Gemini model is set to Flash-Lite. It  is optimized for speed. It is ideal for quick images and basic ideas. It isn’t designed for multiple image references or for multiple edits.
Nano Banana 2:
This option is selected when you make an image request with the Gemini model set to Flash or Pro. It  balances speed with higher image quality, more world knowledge, and reliable text rendering. It can accept multiple reference images.
Nano Banana Pro:
Available with a Google AI Plan, this option is available when you have the Gemini model set to Pro and want  to redo an image when additional detail is needed. It has the highest level of world knowledge, advanced text and language capabilities, higher consistency, and more precise creative control.

Redo an image with Nano Banana Pro
Paid subscribers can use Nano Banana Pro to regenerate an image. Nano Banana Pro can provide additional detail, especially for images that use text rendering or for infographics. To use Nano Banana Pro:
Create an image with Nano Banana 2 or Nano Banana 2 Lite.

If you reach your daily quota of Nano Banana 2 images, you can’t redo any additional images with Nano Banana Pro.

Tips for image generation prompts
Start your prompt with words like draw, generate, and create.
Describe the image style you want. Here are a few examples: photorealistic, charcoal drawing, watercolor painting, cartoon illustration.
Include a detailed visual description of what you want in the image. This can include the subject of the image, what the subject is doing, and the background or setting.
How image generation works with Personal Intelligence
Important:
Nano Banana with Personal Intelligence is only available to users 18 and over in the US. To use this feature, you must sign in to a personal Google Account. The Google Photos connected app currently isn’t available in some US regions.
You can ask Gemini to create or edit highly personalized images using Personal Intelligence. With your permission, Gemini can understand and retrieve info across your Google apps, past chats, and more to understand your unique style, life, and preferences. It then uses the latest Nano Banana model to create and edit images tailored entirely to you.
Try these example prompts:
Create a watercolor image of my dream house nestled in my favorite setting.
Create a claymation image of me and my family enjoying our favorite activity. We want to be standing in the center of the image, staring straight forward.
This example requires Google Photos to be connected to Gemini Apps.
Learn how to connect Google Photos
.
Gemini can use the labels you’ve set up in Google Photos to identify people and pets to generate images of them with Nano Banana.
Tip:
To create images of other people from your Google Photos, make sure their face group is named.
Learn how to set up and manage your face groups
.
Personal Intelligence experiences are optional. You can choose which Google apps to connect, manage how your past chats are used, and set your own instructions.
Learn how to manage Personal Intelligence in Gemini Apps
.
