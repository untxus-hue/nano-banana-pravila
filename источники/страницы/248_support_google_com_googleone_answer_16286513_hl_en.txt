Google AI Ultra benefits
Important:
The benefits of Google AI Ultra include experimental features that use generative AI. Experimental features won't always get it right, and may suggest inaccurate or inappropriate responses that don’t represent Google’s views. When you leave feedback, you can help improve this feature for everyone.
Use Google Antigravity
Google AI Ultra members get the top features for the Google Antigravity platform, a Gemini 3 Pro-powered development environment. This includes first access to new models, prioritized traffic, and the highest usage limits. Ultra access is essential for professional, high-volume agentic development and orchestration.
Highest AI agent quotas:
AI Ultra members get the highest usage limits for Gemini 3 Pro and other Vertex AI Model Garden models (e.g., Claude 4.5 Sonnet, gpt-oss-120b) within the Antigravity platform. AI Ultra members also receive priority access to new, experimental models as capacity permits.
Use AI credits in Google Antigravity
To extend your sessions beyond your baseline quota, use Google AI credits.
Learn how AI credits work in Antigravity
To make sure you have reliable access to models, Antigravity has a two-tiered usage system:
Baseline quota:
Based on your subscription tier, you receive time-bound usage limits specific to Google Antigravity.
Learn more about Google Antigravity
.
AI credits:
When you use up your baseline quota, you can purchase AI credits through Google One to continue usage. These credits can be used across supported Google products. AI credits are deducted based on standard API pricing for the specific model and complexity of request.
Learn more about how AI credits work
.
To check your tier's allocation, you can visit
Google Antigravity Plans
.
Manage your usage settings
To control how and when your credits are spent, go to the "Settings" page or the "Models" selector in Google Antigravity. You can choose from these options:
Toggle for overage:
You can manage extra usage by turning on a toggle to use AI credits for overage when base limits are reached.
Never:
When you reach your baseline quota, you’ll find a "Baseline model quota reached" notification.
Always:
The moment your baseline quota ends, Google Antigravity automatically uses your AI credit balance.
Check your balance
To check your AI usage, go to the app’s "Settings" page or the "Models” selector. The Models selector displays a status that shows your baseline quota consumption.
Tips:
You can purchase AI credits in various increments.
To get more AI credits, go to
one.google.com/ai/credits
.

Only English is supported for prompts and instructions.

Photo to video
You can animate your photo and turn static moments into dynamic short video clips. Photo to video is a new AI feature in Google Photos that brings your photos to life. It uses the latest AI to transform a single photo into a short video with subtle movements or dynamic animations for a unique, cinematic feel.
For transparency, every video generated with Photo to video includes:
A visual watermark that shows the content is AI generated.
An invisible SynthID digital watermark that provides details about the video's origin.
Learn how Photo to video works

The videos are in portrait mode:
If your photo isn't a portrait, it’ll be automatically cropped.
