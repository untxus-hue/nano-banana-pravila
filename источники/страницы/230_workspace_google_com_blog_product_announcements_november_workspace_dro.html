November Workspace Drop: Nano Banana Pro in Slides, Vids, and the Gemini app, plus AI to make meetings more impactful

November 26, 2025
The Google Workspace Team

Try Google Workspace at No Cost

Get a business email, all the storage you need, video conferencing, and more.

SIGN UP

In November’s Workspace Drop, we’re delivering powerful new AI features built with Gemini 3, plus new AI features to help make meetings more effective.

Here’s what’s new:

  • Professional-grade image creation: Introducing Nano Banana Pro in Slides, Vids, the Gemini app, and NotebookLM.
  • Tackle your most ambitious projects: Gemini 3 Pro in the Gemini app to reason across text, images, audio, and video.
  • Uplevel your team collaboration: New Gemini features to improve meeting workflow — before, during, and after.
  • Take your work on the go: AI-powered audio overviews are now available for PDFs in Google Drive.
  • Create high quality videos: Use new ingredients-to-video capabilities in Vids.

Professional-grade image creation: Introducing Nano Banana Pro in Slides, Vids, Gemini app, and NotebookLM

We’re excited to introduce Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image), our new state-of-the-art image generation and editing model, giving more complex and sophisticated outputs and advanced, precise control. Built on Gemini 3 Pro, Nano Banana Pro allows you to blend photos for high-fidelity mockups, design collateral with sharp and accurately placed text, or generate data-rich infographics with a polished look.

Introducing Nano Banana Pro.

With Nano Banana Pro in Slides, you can now create stunning infographics, images, and even complete slides — no matter your design skills. In just a click, Beautify this slide turns your ideas into a compelling visual that maintains the fidelity of your text while modeling the look and feel of the overall deck. For more control, use the updated Help me visualize sidebar to enter more detailed prompts and select from specific output styles (slide, image, infographic), then refine your results with built-in advanced image editing capabilities. The new infographic option generates eye-catching visualizations of your content by merging state-of-the-art text rendering and Gemini 3 Pro’s advanced reasoning.

Beautify your slides with Nano Banana Pro in Slides.

Tackle your most ambitious projects: Gemini 3 Pro in the Gemini app to reasoning across text, images, audio and video

We’re bringing our most intelligent model yet, Gemini 3 Pro, to the Gemini app for Workspace customers. This update sets a new bar for AI model performance and delivers a more powerful and helpful experience, with responses that are clearer and easier to read. Gemini 3 Pro has improved reasoning capabilities across text, images, and charts compared to previous models, helping you analyze data or brainstorm creative ideas.

Introducing Gemini 3 Pro.

Uplevel your team collaboration: New Gemini features to improve meeting workflow – before, during, and after.

We’re rolling out powerful new Gemini features across Gmail, Meet, and Chat to make your meetings smoother, from beginning to end.

Help me schedule in Gmail will suggest meeting times based on your Google Calendar and the context of the email, in a single step, right from your inbox.

"Help me schedule" in Gmail.

During the meeting, Ask Gemini in Meet for real-time help. Gemini can catch you up on what you missed, share key takeaways, or recap a discussion detail.

Gemini in Meet providing a summary of the meeting up to this point.

Continuous meeting chat brings in-meeting messages and shared artifacts into a persistent Google Chat conversation, so teams can move from planning into action without missing a beat.

Stay connected after the meeting ends with continuous meeting chat.

Take your work on the go: AI-powered audio overviews for PDFs in Google Drive

You can now use Gemini in Google Drive to convert long, text-heavy PDFs into a conversational, podcast-style audio summary. Whether you’re commuting, working out, or doing chores, you can listen to summaries from anywhere you can access your Drive files. Once an audio overview is generated on a desktop, it will automatically be saved to a new “Audio overviews” folder in your Drive, where you can then access it from any mobile or desktop device.

Boost efficiency, improve accessibility, and enhance preparation with audio overviews for PDFs in Google Drive.

Create high quality videos: Use new ingredients-to-video capabilities in Vids

The new ingredients-to-video capability of Veo 3.1 in Vids now lets you generate an 8-second clip using up to three different images and a prompt. Whether you’re creating a brand promotion, training video, or employee onboarding video, you can now create clips with consistent subjects, characters, and objects.

Create video clips from images with Veo 3.1 in Vids.

Get started today

All these updates are rolling out to Google Workspace customers now. You can take advantage of these updates today, either with a no-cost trial or included with any of our business plans.

We’ll be taking a break from Workspace Drops for December, and we look forward to sharing more new features from the Google Workspace team in January 2026.
