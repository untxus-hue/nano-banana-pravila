Welcome to Google Cloud Next ‘26

When we gathered at Next a year ago, we talked about how generative AI was transforming organizations around the world. Today, that future is in-production–the Agentic Enterprise is real–-and deployed at a scale the world has never before seen. At Next, we show you everything you need to make your organization an Agentic Enterprise.

This past year, we have been relentlessly focused on delivering innovation that drives your growth, introducing thousands of new capabilities across our portfolio. Our seventh-generation Ironwood TPUs and our new Axion custom processors are now generally available, providing customers with purpose-built, cost-effective infrastructure designed for the Agentic Enterprise. We launched groundbreaking new foundational models, including Gemini 3.1 Pro and our new image generation model, Nano Banana 2. And we delivered Gemini Enterprise, bringing the best of Google AI to every employee. Instead of piecing together a collection of fragmented systems, we offer a vertically optimized stack where everything is co-developed.

All of this innovation is propelling customer adoption. In fact, nearly 75% of Google Cloud customers are using our AI products to power their businesses. In addition, over the past 12 months, 330 Google Cloud customers each processed more than one trillion tokens, while 35 reached the 10-trillion-token milestone with our models. Our first-party models now process more than 16 billion tokens per minute via direct API use by our customers, up from 10 billion last quarter. 

Today, organizations are choosing Google Cloud for three primary reasons:

  1. Fully integrated AI stack: Organizations partner with us for our deep-rooted expertise and our ability to deliver first-party solutions across the entire AI stack. This isn't about offering individual services that can be cobbled together; it is about providing a comprehensive backbone for innovation. Our full stack approach across AI infrastructure; leading models like Gemini; data management capabilities; enterprise-grade foundation; developer tools and platform; and agents and applications make us unique in the market.

  2. Secure-by-design, enterprise-ready foundation: Our customers trust Google Cloud because we provide a secure, unified platform built on Zero Trust security and planet-scale reliability. This is the same foundation that supports Google’s most demanding global services. Not only is our platform secure by default, but we also offer leading intel-driven and AI-powered cybersecurity solutions. These integrate development and security operations across hybrid and multicloud environments to effectively prevent, detect, and respond to threats up and down the entire AI stack.

  3. Gemini Enterprise, the front door to AI for every employee and customer: Gemini Enterprise enables every customer and employee to build, use, deploy, and manage cutting-edge AI agents. Whether it's bringing together sales and service interactions to improve customer touchpoints, automating complex workflows to improve your supply chain efficiency, or enabling marketers to prototype and create campaigns in record time, Gemini Enterprise is transforming the way companies get work done.

Today at Next ‘26, we are announcing everything you need to make your organization an Agentic Enterprise – all anchored by a unified stack designed to turn intelligence into a growth engine for your business. Gemini Enterprise is now the end-to-end system for the Agentic Era—the connective tissue between your data, your people, and all of your apps and agents that transforms all of your processes into a single, intelligent flow. New innovations announced today include:

  • Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform is our new, comprehensive platform to build, scale, govern, and optimize agents. Built on top of our leading infrastructure, and deeply integrated with our data and security capabilities, it is the foundation for the Agentic Enterprise. It brings together the best of Vertex AI with transformational new features, including Agent Studio, Agent-to-Agent Orchestration, Agent Registry, Agent Identity, Agent Gateway, Agent Observability, and much, much more. This empowers your technical teams with a comprehensive, unified platform to manage every aspect of your agents, and deliver them through the Gemini Enterprise app, the front door for AI for every employee.

  • Gemini Enterprise app includes new features that let every employee build with AI, including a new Agent Designer that helps build sophisticated schedule- or trigger-based agents, long-running agents to execute complex business processes, an Inbox for managing agent activity, Skills to create shortcuts for repetitive tasks, and Canvas to create and edit files without switching applications.

  • AI Infrastructure developments include our eighth-generation TPUs, with TPU 8t for accelerated training and TPU 8i for cost-effective, near-zero latency inference, along with storage innovations like Managed Lustre with 10 TB per second of throughput, and networking advancements like Virgo Networking for high-performance at massive scale.

We are announcing Knowledge Catalog to ground agents in trusted business context across your entire data estate, a Data Agent Kit for Gemini-powered data science authoring, and a cross-cloud, AI-native Lakehouse that gives you seamless access to all of your data, regardless of where it’s located, unleashing a borderless foundation for autonomous action.

Gemini Enterprise: Bringing the Agentic Enterprise to life

Gemini Enterprise is seeing tremendous momentum. In Q1, we saw 40% growth in paid monthly active users quarter-over-quarter. And today we are announcing Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, which has everything your technical teams need to build and orchestrate agents. It brings together the model selection, model building, and tuning services of Vertex AI that customers love, along with new features for agent integration, security, DevOps, orchestration, and more. It also includes the Gemini Enterprise app, which brings the best of Google AI to every employee, for every workflow. 

Agent Platform provides first-class access to the world’s leading models, including Gemini 3.1 Pro—our most advanced model, optimized for complex workflow orchestration; Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, also known as Nano Banana 2, to generate high-fidelity user interfaces and visual assets; Lyria 3—our state-of-the-art model for generating professional-grade audio and music; and leading models from Anthropic, including Claude Opus, Sonnet and Haiku. And today we are also adding support for Anthropic’s Claude Opus 4.7.

The platform is designed around four core pillars—build, scale, govern, and optimize—that allow teams to collaborate seamlessly, and we are announcing a number of new features that allow you to:

Build agents quickly and easily by empowering your developers, business users, and everyone in-between to build and deploy agents at scale. 

  • Agent Developer Kit (ADK) has a new graph-based framework that allows you to define clear, reliable logic for how agents work together to solve complex problems. 

  • Agent Studio, our new low-code interface, enables every developer to build, test, and publish agents using natural language. 

  • Agent Registry provides a single point of control, indexing every internal agent and tool across your organization to ensure they are discoverable and governed. 

  • Ecosystem Integrations via Agent Marketplace help instantly deploy specialized agents from partners like Atlassian, Box, Lovable, Oracle, ServiceNow, Workday, and many more—directly from Gemini Enterprise. Also, you can leverage new integrations that enable agents from partners that use a unified Agent Registry with reusable skills and managed environments to treat all protocols as first-class citizens.

  • Model Context Protocol allows you to connect our Agent Platform to any MCP Server. We are also exposing Google Cloud Services as MCP to allow you to interact seamlessly with Google Cloud from any agent.

Scale and orchestrate agents through a modular set of services that can be used together or independently to streamline the path from agent proof-of-concept to production via our highly scalable and secure container for running agents in production.

  • Agent-to-Agent Orchestration enables agents to seamlessly delegate tasks to one another, including support for complex, generative, and deterministic orchestration patterns. This ensures that for critical flows such as compliance, your agents can follow well-specified paths every time. 
  • Runtime improvements deliver the immediate responsiveness your users expect with a revamped foundation that delivers sub-second "cold starts" and the ability to provision new agents in seconds. It also helps reduce the overhead of idle infrastructure.
  • Support for long-running agents enables your agents to sustain autonomous reasoning and manage multi-step workflows.
  • Secure sandboxes provide isolated environments for agents to execute model-generated code and perform computer use tasks like browser-based automation—without risk to your host systems.
  • Memory Bank and Memory Profiles give your agents scalable, long-term memory that replaces temporary sessions with persistent context. Agents can recall specific user details and project histories over months to deliver deeply personalized interactions.
  • Agent Sessions enhance developer flexibility by allowing the use of unique identifiers for precise session tracking across disparate enterprise systems.

Govern agents with our secure-by-design architecture that allows you to manage a fleet of agents with the same rigor you apply to other mission-critical systems.

  • Agent Identity improves the security posture of your agents by ensuring every agent receives a unique cryptographic ID and well defined authorization policies that are traceable and auditable. With Agent Identify, agents are governed through your enterprise policies, stay within assigned tasks, and ask a human for help when a situation gets too complex.

  • Agent Gateway provides the air traffic control for your agent ecosystem. It understands agentic protocols (MCP, A2A, etc.) and provides centralized, real-time policy enforcement—helping to ensure every interaction between your agents and your data is secure, compliant, and scalable by design. Integrated inline protection with Model Armor safeguards your agent interactions against prompt injection, tool poisoning, and sensitive data leakage.

  • Agent Anomaly Detection proactively flags suspicious behavior before it impacts your business. By analyzing the intent behind agent actions, it automatically detects risks, such as tool misuse, unauthorized data access, and reasoning drift that can lead to unintended outcomes.

  • Agent Security dashboard gives you a full view of AI risks and compliance using the Security Command Center. It allows your teams to map relationships between agents and models, automate asset discovery, and scan for vulnerabilities in the underlying operating system and language packages.

Optimize agents using data-driven feedback loops to improve accuracy, reduce latency, and control costs, helping to ensure every AI agent performs exactly as intended.

  • Agent Observability delivers turnkey dashboards with automated logging and agent auditing for total oversight. Using standardized, OTel-compliant telemetry, you can now verify every agent, tool, and API handoff to ensure accountability, visualize full execution paths, and quickly diagnose reasoning loops or monitor performance metrics.
  • Agent Simulation lets you stress-test agents at scale against human-like synthetic interactions in a controlled environment. Generating thousands of synthetic, multi-step customer interactions, Agent Simulation rigorously evaluates logic and conversation flow. 
  • Agent Evaluation continuously scores agent performance against live production traffic using multi-turn autoraters to detect quality degradation and anomalies in real-time.

Gemini Enterprise app has all the key components to let teams discover, create, share, and run AI agents in a single environment. Today we are introducing several new capabilities in the Gemini Enterprise app:

  • Agent Designer uses the same no-code agent designer experience of Agent Platform and lets employees build sophisticated schedule- or trigger-based agents using any enterprise connector. It gives you a virtual flowchart of your agent, allowing you to inspect, test, and approve workflows, ensuring total transparency for executing critical business processes.  

  • Long-running agents are designed to execute complex business processes. They can work autonomously in secure cloud sandboxes, giving agents the ability to orchestrate business logic, write code to build custom tools, and complete multi-step work like reconciliation activities or sales prospect sequencing—without needing constant prompting. 

  • Inbox in Gemini Enterprise provides a central location to monitor, guide, and help manage all of your agent activity, including your long-running agents. Notifications are intuitively categorized into actionable groups like "Needs your input," "Errors," and "Completed.” 

  • Projects create a dedicated space where the agent’s memory is confined to the files and conversations your team adds. By connecting it to data sources like Google Drive, NotebookLM, and Google Group Chats, the agent becomes an expert on a specific topic and can provide team members daily briefings or status updates without digging through months of documents.

  • Skills create simple shortcuts using an “@” mention for repetitive tasks like applying brand guidelines, formatting a report, or accessing specific data.

  • Canvas gives our customers an interactive editor directly within Gemini Enterprise. It allows teams to easily create and edit Docs and Slides, and even export to Microsoft 365 files, within the same experience. 

  • Agent Gallery provides access to third-party agents from partners like Adobe, Atlassian, Lovable, and ServiceNow, and is adding more third-party connectors for Asana, Mailchimp, Workday, and more. These integrations enable your agents to retrieve data and execute tasks with your systems-of-record.

The foundation for the Agentic Enterprise: AI Hypercomputer

Designed specifically for demanding AI workloads, our AI Hypercomputer is an advanced, purpose-built architecture that unites performance-optimized hardware for compute, storage, networking, open software and ML frameworks—as well as flexible consumption models—

into a single, integrated system. Our ability to co-evolve all of these components translates to leading power and performance efficiency for our customers. 

At the heart of this system is the industry’s widest variety of compute options, spanning our custom Google TPUs and Axion CPUs, to the latest NVIDIA GPUs, giving customers the ultimate flexibility to pick the right architecture for the job without compromise.

Today we are announcing innovations at every layer of the AI Hypercomputer:

  • We offer the widest array of compute processors to customers, and our portfolio is expanding today to include: 

    • TPU 8t, optimized for training, uses breakthrough Inter-Chip Interconnect (ICI) technology to scale up to 9,600 TPUs and 2 PB of shared, high-bandwidth memory in a single superpod. It achieves 3x the processing power of Ironwood and delivers up to 2x more performance/Watt. 

    • TPU 8i, optimized for inference, uses our new Boardfly topology to directly connect 1,152 TPUs in a single pod. It features 3x more on-chip SRAM compared to previous versions to host larger KV caches entirely on-silicon and integrates a specialized Collectives Acceleration Engine. Taken together, TPU 8i delivers 80% better performance per dollar for inference than the prior generation, enabling millions of concurrent agents to run cost-effectively.

    • NVIDIA GPUs are a core part of our AI accelerator portfolio. We will be among the first to offer NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72, in addition to the Blackwell- and Hopper-based instances already available. 

    • Google Cloud Axion includes our N4A Axion instances—launched in January—which deliver 100% better price-performance than comparable x86 instances, ensuring sustained, efficient operation for agents. 

    • Network-optimized Compute is expanding with new C4N and M4N machine series, perfectly optimized for high-volume agent communication, network-heavy Telco 5G core, and enterprise databases. C4N instances can deliver an almost 4x increase in network bandwidth-per-vCPU over standard C4 instances.

  • New innovations in Storage include improvements in Managed Lustre, which now delivers 10 TB per second of throughput to A5X or TPU 8t over RDMA, supercharging training. In Rapid Storage, we’ve made significant performance improvements—going from 6 TB/sec to 15 TB/sec—to speed up AI training and inference workloads. And we are introducing Smart Storage, which applies semantic meaning to unstructured data, providing the foundation for our Enterprise Knowledge Graph.

  • New capabilities in Networking include the Virgo Network, our new, purpose-built, AI-optimized network that connects either NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 systems or TPU 8t superpods into massive supercomputers with hundreds of thousands of accelerators, supercharging distributed training of the world's most capable frontier models.

  • We are also announcing new features in our Compute Services, including the expansion of our OSS Frameworks and Inference Engines to include native support for Pytorch for TPUs, as well as optimized vLLM support across GPUs and TPUs. And Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) has new capabilities that deliver dramatically faster cold starts and scale-out for AI inference workloads, and new agent sandboxes that are capable of deploying 300 sandboxes per second, per cluster, with sub-second time to first instruction.

In this agentic era, you can’t have humans manually filing tickets to manage capacity or troubleshoot configurations; you need a cloud that drives itself. We’ve used the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to turn every Google Cloud service into a tool that agents can orchestrate directly, enabling them to troubleshoot our own infrastructure using decades of our own telemetry. And by integrating Gemini reasoning into our telemetry libraries, the system now performs autonomous root-cause analysis—identifying misconfigurations and remediations to fix before a human even realizes there’s a problem.

And of course, Google uses this infrastructure to power our own models, including Gemini and our portfolio of media generation models—driving new levels of efficiency, creativity, and customer engagement. By designing the model and the machine in tandem, we eliminate the constraints of fragmented stacks to deliver extreme scale and efficiency, as well as leading price-performance. In addition, this ensures that breakthroughs achieved through our research are immediately ready for the rigors of production for our customers.

The Agentic Data Cloud: A new data architecture built for the speed and scale of agentic AI

The Agentic Enterprise demands a fundamental shift in data architecture. For a decade, "systems of intelligence" functioned as reactive data archives built for human scale. Today, we are moving to “systems of action” built for proactive, trusted agents that run at agent scale. The Agentic Data Cloud delivers an AI-native architecture—a unified foundation that frees your data from the silos of the last generation—allowing agents to perceive, reason, and act on your behalf in real-time.

New capabilities in the Agentic Data Cloud we are announcing today include:

  • Cross-Cloud Lakehouse, standardized on Apache Iceberg, is our Lakehouse that enables you to leave your data in AWS or Azure (coming later this year) while querying it instantly—without the friction of vendor lock-in or the cost of data movement. We provide zero-copy access to applications, operating systems, and AI platforms, such as Databricks, Palantir, Salesforce Data360, SAP, ServiceNow, Snowflake, Workday, and more.

  • Lightning Engine for Apache Spark delivers a real-time, serverless engine that is up to 4.5x faster than open-source alternatives and offers up to 2x better price-performance over the leading competitor for large datasets.

  • Data Agent Kit delivers a Gemini-powered data science authoring experience across your IDEs, Notebooks, and Agentic Terminals. It enables data practitioners with intent-driven development in Python, Spark, and SQL—simply state your business goal, and the Data Agent Kit handles the rest. 

  • Knowledge Catalog constructs a unified, dynamic context graph of your entire business enabling you to ground agents in all of your business data and semantics. With Smart Storage and the Object Context API, files in Google Cloud Storage are instantly tagged and enriched with metadata before an agent touches them. Then our Knowledge Engine uses Gemini to autonomously tag, define logic and instantly map complex relationships across your entire enterprise, providing the semantic definition your agents have been missing. 

  • Deep Research Agent for enterprise insight is an autonomous agent that combines research and analytical skills. While Deep Research in Gemini Enterprise already works with your documents and SaaS systems to synthesize your business data, it now connects to data platforms like BigQuery, ensuring a full picture of all your structured and unstructured data. Now it can answer complex business questions like “what is the root cause of my supply chain disruption?” or "can you perform a financial audit on this region?” and include citations and precision that previously required weeks of manual effort.

Protecting the Agentic Enterprise: Security built for the AI era

Companies across industries face a compounding security challenge. As they expand into complex hybrid and multicloud environments, their attack surface is growing. At the same time, organizations are rapidly deploying AI agents—feeding them sensitive, proprietary data to streamline core business operations. Meanwhile, adversaries are weaponizing AI to launch faster, more sophisticated attacks directly targeting these new enterprise models and the critical data powering them.

To combat this, human analysts need AI on their side. We offer an AI-powered cybersecurity platform that combines Google’s Threat Intelligence and Security Operations with Wiz’s Cloud and AI Security Platform to prevent, detect, and respond to threats across all environments. Customers are already benefiting from our Triage and Investigation Agent, which has processed more than 5 million alerts to date, reducing a typical 30-minute manual analysis to 60 seconds.

Today at Next we are announcing a number of new solutions for agentic defense:

  • Agentic SecOps: Supercharging agentic defense with threat intelligence

    • Dark Web Intelligence builds a nuanced profile of your organization using the newest Gemini models, based on Google Threat Intelligence Group’s dark web expertise. Internal tests show it can analyze millions of daily external events with 98% accuracy to help elevate only the threats that truly matter to your organization.

    • Threat Hunting Agent uses Google’s massive threat intelligence knowledge to allow teams to proactively hunt for novel attack patterns and adversary behaviors that bypass traditional defenses.

    • Detection Engineering Agent generates persistent detection rules for threat scenarios, transforming detection creation from a manual craft into an automated process.

  • Wiz: Protecting AI and cloud apps across any infrastructure

    • Wiz AI Application Protection Platform (AI-APP) secures every layer of AI applications—infrastructure, data, access, models, agents, and applications—from code to runtime, across every environment where you build. AI-APP brings together visibility, risk analysis, and runtime protection into a single, graph-powered platform that helps teams understand and secure AI systems as they operate. Today, we’re taking our commitment further. Wiz now supports Databricks, as well as new agent studios like AWS Agentcore, Gemini Enterprise Agent Builder, Microsoft Azure Copilot Studio, and Salesforce Agentforce so customers gain protection however their teams choose to build. Wiz now also connects to the outer layer of the cloud, including Apigee, Cloudflare, Vercel, and others to add more context on the external attack surface.

    • Wiz Red Agent acts like a sophisticated security researcher, but at AI speed and scale. It can reason about application behavior, adapt its approach in real time, and validate exploitable risks across your web applications and APIs, empowering you to stay one step ahead of attackers.

    • Wiz Blue Agent gathers evidence across cloud telemetry, runtime signals, and identity context to investigate a threat and assess its potential severity, providing its full investigation logic so you can resolve threats quickly.

    • Wiz Green Agent acts as a built-in investigation and remediation engine, continuously analyzing your highest-risk issues. Like a seasoned security engineer, it synthesizes context from across Wiz—including the Security Graph, code-to-cloud relationships, identity ownership, and historical remediation patterns—to identify the true root cause of a risk and the safest, most effective resolution. Teams get environment-specific, step-by-step remediation guidance so fixes are durable.

    • Wiz Agentic Workflows introduces a new hub for orchestrating cloud and AI security in Wiz. It brings together the rich context of Wiz, analysis from Wiz AI agents, and a customizable drag-and-drop interface. Together, this enables security teams to define how work gets done, from when and how agents act to where human input is required. 

In addition, we are introducing Google Cloud Fraud Defense, a comprehensive platform designed to discern the legitimacy and authorization of bots, humans, and agents. Fraud Defense is the evolution of reCAPTCHA, and provides the intelligence that businesses need to trust their digital interactions and commerce.
