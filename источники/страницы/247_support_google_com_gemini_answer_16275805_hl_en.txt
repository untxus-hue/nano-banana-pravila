Gemini Apps limits & upgrades for Google AI subscribers
Notification
Starting on May 17, 2026 there will be changes to usage limits for Gemini Apps.
Learn more about these changes
.
Gemini Apps have compute-based usage limits that determine how much you can interact with Gemini tools and features. These limits factor in the complexity of your prompt, the models and features you use, and the length of your chat. Your limit refreshes every 5 hours until you reach your weekly limit.
You can upgrade to a Google AI plan for expanded access to features and models in Gemini Apps. Gemini Apps upgrades are part of select Google One paid plans for personal accounts.
Learn more about availalble subscriptions
.
Important:
This article is for users managing Gemini Apps for a personal Google Account. If you’re using a work or school Google Account,
learn more about using Gemini Apps with a work or school account
.
What you need
To use Gemini Apps with a Google AI plan in most countries, you must be 13 (or
the applicable age in your country
) or over.
To use Gemini Apps with a Google AI plan in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom, you must be 18 years of age or older.
Some features may not be available for users under the age of 18 or 13 (or
the applicable age in your country
). In the help center, you will find additional information about age requirements.

Gemini app access and limits
Important:
Gemini Apps limits may change. Access is subject to change or may be limited based on testing, experimentation, or availability.
Gemini Apps usage limits are designed to ensure an optimal experience for everyone. This means we may at times have to cap the number of prompts and conversations you can have, or how much you can use some features, within a specific timeframe.

About Gemini 3 models
Important:
Model names, versions, and availability may change.
Gemini has the following available models:
Gemini Flash-Lite
is an efficient workhorse model designed for speed. It's ideal for everyday tasks like summarization and brainstorming.
Gemini Flash
is a more powerful model that balances speed and reasoning to solve a large variety of problems, from simple to complex.
Gemini Pro
is our most advanced model that excels at complex math and coding prompts with high performance and reasoning. Pro has a deeper understanding across text, files, images and videos, plus major upgrades for coding and studying. Generally, response time for Gemini 3 Pro prompts will take longer than if using other models.
Thinking level
Where available, you can adjust the thinking level for Gemini models.
Standard thinking
is the default and best for most questions. With Standard thinking, responses will be generally faster.
Extended thinking
is best for complex problem solving. With Extended thinking, the model will reason over your prompt longer before responding.
Deep think
(AI Ultra only) provides maximum parallel reasoning. Deep Think queries generally can take a few minutes before seeing a response. Deep think requires the Pro model.
Tip:
More advanced models and higher thinking levels consume more of your usage.

Context windows
When you upload files to Gemini Apps, it needs to "read" the content to provide useful insights or answers. The context window is like the reading capacity. It determines how much content can be understood together at one time. A larger context window allows Gemini Apps to work with longer prompts and chats.

More info about limits
What you can do when you reach a usage limit
If you have a Google AI subscription and reach your limit, you can continue your conversation with Flash-Lite.
If you reach your five hour or weekly usage limits you can upgrade to a Google AI subscription with higher limits or wait until your model limit is refreshed.
Learn how to upgrade
.
Usage limit changes
Limits may change without notice, including due to capacity constraints. When there’s a large increase in activity in Gemini Apps, we may change limits to maintain a high standard of quality.
Important:
For users without a Google AI plan, some features that require more compute resources, like Deep Research, may be unavailable during periods of high demand.
If capacity changes, limits for users without a Google AI plan may be limited before users with a plan.
How to tell if you’re close to a limit & when it refreshes
Gemini Apps will notify you when you're close to your limit based on your plan's level. When you reach your limit, Gemini Apps gives you another notification that tells you when your limit will refresh.
To view your usage limits:
Visit
gemini.google.com
.
At the bottom left, select Settings
Usage Limits
.
Why you might want a larger context window
A larger context window allows Gemini to read and comprehend more. For example, with a 1M token context window, Gemini can understand up to 1,500 pages of text or 30,000 lines of code.
If you exceed the context window, this could lead to responses that don’t take into account all the content provided or miss connections or details throughout the content. This is most relevant for prompts that require attention to many details scattered throughout large files.
